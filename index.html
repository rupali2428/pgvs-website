About Us

Prakashani Gram Evam Nagar Vikas Sansthan (PGVS) is a registered non-profit organization established in 1996–1997 with Registration Number 1734. Founded by Dr. Gaytri Srivastava, the organization is dedicated to improving the lives of rural and urban communities through education, healthcare, women empowerment, skill development, environmental conservation, child welfare and sustainable development.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower rural and urban communities through education, healthcare, women empowerment, environmental protection, skill development and sustainable social development.

Our Vision

To build an educated, healthy, self-reliant and inclusive society where every individual enjoys equal opportunities and lives with dignity.

Our Objectives

  • Promote education and literacy among children, youth and adults.
  • Empower women through awareness and skill development programmes.
  • Improve public health through health camps and awareness campaigns.
  • Support sustainable rural and urban development.
  • Generate employment and livelihood opportunities.
  • Promote environmental protection and tree plantation.
  • Protect the rights of children, women and senior citizens.
  • Create awareness about government welfare schemes.
  • Organize social, educational and cultural programmes.
  • Promote equality, peace and community participation.

100 Days National Campaign Against Child Marriage

📅 09 January 2026

📍 Kalpnath Singh Kanya Inter College

PGVS, in collaboration with Manav Sansadhan and Mahila Vikas Sansthan, organized this awareness programme under the 100 Days National Campaign Against Child Marriage to promote women's rights, gender equality, and prevent child marriage. The campaign was conducted over two days at two different locations, including Kalpnath Singh Kanya Inter College, where students, teachers, and community members actively participated in spreading awareness about the harmful effects of child marriage and the importance of girls' education, safety, and empowerment.

Chief Guest:

• Mr. Virendra Mishra, Member, Child Welfare Committee (CWC), Siddharthnagar

• Mr. Dilip Kumar Soni, Station House Officer (SHO)

Organized by PGVS in collaboration with Manav Sansadhan and Mahila Vikas Sansthan.

Our Contribution Towards the 17 Sustainable Development Goals

👨‍👩‍👧 No Poverty

PGVS supports vulnerable families through awareness, livelihood promotion and government welfare scheme linkage to reduce poverty and improve quality of life.

🍚 Zero Hunger

We promote nutrition awareness, maternal health, child nutrition and community participation for a healthier society.

❤️ Good Health & Well-being

Our health programmes focus on immunization, sanitation, disease prevention, maternal care and community health awareness.

📚 Quality Education

We encourage quality education, digital literacy and equal learning opportunities for children and youth.

⚧ Gender Equality

PGVS empowers women and girls through awareness, leadership development, legal education and skill training.

💧 Clean Water & Sanitation

We promote hygiene awareness, sanitation practices and safe drinking water for healthier communities.

☀️ Affordable & Clean Energy

PGVS encourages sustainable energy awareness and responsible use of natural resources in rural communities.

📈 Decent Work & Economic Growth

We organize livelihood programmes, vocational training and entrepreneurship opportunities for sustainable income generation.

🏗️ Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure

PGVS promotes innovation, digital inclusion and skill development to strengthen community growth.

🤝 Reduced Inequalities

PGVS works to reduce social and economic inequalities by ensuring equal opportunities, inclusion, and support for disadvantaged communities.

🏙️ Sustainable Cities & Communities

We promote clean, safe, and sustainable communities through awareness, environmental conservation, and active public participation.

♻️ Responsible Consumption & Production

PGVS encourages responsible use of natural resources, waste reduction, recycling, and environmentally friendly practices.

🌍 Climate Action

Through tree plantation, environmental awareness, and climate education, we inspire communities to protect nature and reduce climate risks.

🐟 Life Below Water

We promote awareness about protecting rivers, ponds, and water resources by preventing pollution and encouraging responsible water use.

🌳 Life on Land

PGVS supports biodiversity conservation, tree plantation, forest protection, and sustainable management of natural resources.

⚖️ Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions

We promote legal awareness, child protection, women's rights, transparency, and peaceful, inclusive communities.

🤲 Partnerships for the Goals

PGVS collaborates with government departments, educational institutions, NGOs, health professionals, and community stakeholders to achieve sustainable development goals together.
